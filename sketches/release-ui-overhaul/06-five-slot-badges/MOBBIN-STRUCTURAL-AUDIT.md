## What changed structurally

The rejected v35 retained the same screen grammar and mostly adjusted color, title scale, icon boxes, and density. v36 changes composition by destination:

- **Alarm:** the next enabled alarm is now a dominant immersive wake stage; later alarms remain compact utility rows.
- **Journal:** history is now a date-rail editorial archive with excerpts and explicit entry affordances, not a repeated two-line list.
- **Capture:** capture now occupies a full dark chamber with action and recovery controls anchored toward the thumb zone.
- **Insights:** a single recall-index stage establishes hierarchy before the calendar and contributor ledger; metric icon tiles are removed.
- **Profile:** identity is quieter and settings use unboxed native rows.
- **Navigation:** the icon-only bar is a compact floating dock; the center action no longer towers over the regular tabs.

## Transferable principles

- One dominant surface per screen instead of several equal cards.
- Editorial, sentence-first journaling with generous unboxed space.
- Dark immersive state surfaces reserved for emotionally important moments.
- Data hierarchy led by one meaningful index, then compact contributors.
- Smaller navigation chrome and restrained selected state.
- Typography and spacing carry hierarchy before icons and containers.

## Explicit non-copying boundary

No Mobbin screenshot, asset, proprietary brand mark, exact layout, copy, or distinctive chart is shipped. Dreamworld uses original botanical geometry, its own palette, its own journal/capture information architecture, and its existing functional state model.
